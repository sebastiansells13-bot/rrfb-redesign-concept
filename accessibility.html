Accessibility Statement

Our goal is that everyone can use this site, regardless of ability or the technology they use to browse it.

Our standard

This site is designed and built to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the widely-used benchmark for web accessibility. That includes:

  • Text and background color combinations that meet minimum contrast ratios
  • A visible "skip to content" link for keyboard users
  • Full keyboard navigation — every link, button, form field, and the FAQ accordion can be operated without a mouse
  • Semantic landmarks (header, nav, main, footer) and heading structure for screen-reader navigation
  • Descriptive labels on every form field, and ARIA attributes on interactive widgets like the mobile menu and accordion
  • Text that resizes properly, and layouts that reflow rather than requiring horizontal scrolling

Known limitations

This is a concept build, not a finished production site. Before launch, we'd recommend a full manual audit including screen-reader testing (NVDA/JAWS/VoiceOver), and a review of any content added later (photos need alt text, videos need captions, PDFs need to be tagged).

Report an issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please let us know — the details you give us (the page, what happened, and the browser/assistive technology you were using) help us fix it faster. Reach us at info@rrfb.org, by phone at 505.247.2052, or through our contact form.
