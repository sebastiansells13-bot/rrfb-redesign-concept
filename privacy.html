Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 (template — pending legal review)

Note: this page is a starting template for the redesign pitch, not a finished legal document. Before publishing, an attorney should confirm it matches what the organization actually collects and how it's actually used.

What we collect

When you visit this site, request food assistance, donate, sign up to volunteer, or subscribe to our newsletter, we may collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, phone number, mailing address, and donation or volunteer preferences — along with standard technical information (browser type, pages visited, approximate location from IP address) collected automatically through analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To process donations and send tax receipts
  • To schedule and coordinate volunteer shifts
  • To send requested newsletters or program updates
  • To connect you with the nearest partner agency when you request food assistance
  • To understand site usage and improve the experience

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with partner agencies, payment processors, and service providers strictly to deliver the services above, under agreements that limit their use of your data to that purpose.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email list at any time using the link in that email, or by contacting us directly. You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by reaching out through the contact page.

Cookies & analytics

This site would use cookies for basic functionality and, if analytics are enabled, to understand aggregate traffic patterns. No cookies are used for third-party advertising. A cookie consent banner should be added before analytics go live in a jurisdiction that requires it.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental involvement.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@rrfb.org or through our contact form.
