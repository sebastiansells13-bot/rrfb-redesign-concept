Three ways to help solve hunger

Every gift moves a meal closer to a neighbor.

However you're able to give, it becomes part of the same supply chain: sourced, sorted, and shipped to a pantry near the people who need it.

Give Funds

A single dollar can be stretched into roughly five meals through bulk purchasing and rescued food. Monthly giving keeps trucks moving all year.
