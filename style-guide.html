Spacing & radius

Section vertical rhythm: 88px desktop / 56px mobile. Container max-width: 1180px. Corner radii: 8px (sm, chips) · 16px (md, cards) · 28px (lg, hero bands, feature cards).

Accessibility baseline

  • Body text and interactive-element contrast targets WCAG AA (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text/UI)
  • All focus states use a visible 2px outline (see :focus-visible rules in style.css)
  • Every icon-only control has an aria-label; decorative SVGs are aria-hidden
