Mobile Pantry · September 2, 2026

Fall mobile distribution schedule now open statewide

New stops added across rural counties for the fall distribution season, with expanded hours in three border counties.

Starting this month, Roadrunner Food Bank is expanding its mobile pantry program to reach more rural communities across New Mexico ahead of the winter months, when need typically rises alongside heating costs and school breaks.

The mobile pantry — a truck stocked with fresh produce, protein, and shelf-stable staples — travels to communities that don't have a fixed-location food pantry nearby. This fall's schedule adds new stops in three counties and expands hours at four existing sites, based on turnout data from the past year.

What's new this season

  • Three new rural stops, added based on partner-agency referrals and community requests
  • Extended evening hours at four sites to reach working families
  • A bilingual (English/Spanish) text-message reminder system piloted at five locations

"Every new stop means fewer people driving forty-five minutes for groceries," said a Roadrunner Food Bank distribution coordinator. "We built this season's schedule directly from what our partner agencies told us they were seeing on the ground."

Find a stop near you

Search the food finder on our Get Help page by ZIP code to see the closest mobile distribution date, time, and address. No ID or proof of income is required to attend.
